{{NAME}} is a free RSS reader for iPhone and Android. Follow the sites and blogs you choose, read their articles offline, and bring your subscriptions over from another reader with an OPML file. There are no ads, no account and no tracking.

Why hafnium? Element 72 was discovered in Copenhagen in 1923, at Niels Bohr’s institute, and named after Hafnia, the city’s Latin name. Copenhagen itself means “merchants’ harbour”: a place where things arrive and are kept safe until you want them. That is what this app does with the articles you follow.
