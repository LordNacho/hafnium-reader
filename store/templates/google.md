## Full Description

{{NAME}} is a simple RSS reader for the sites, blogs and news sources you choose. There are no ads, no account to create and nothing to sign in to. Articles are saved on your phone, so you can keep reading on a train, on a plane, or anywhere your connection drops.

Already using another reader? Export your subscriptions as an OPML file from The Old Reader, Feedly, Inoreader or most other services, and import it into {{NAME}}. Your folders come along too. You can export your list again at any time from the share sheet.

Adding a single feed is easy: paste a feed address, or just the site's homepage, and {{NAME}} will look for its feed. It reads RSS 2.0, RSS 1.0 (RDF) and Atom.

Reading
• A clean article view with previous and next, star, mark read or unread, and share
• Open the original page in the in-app browser
• System, light, dark or sepia theme
• Text size from 85% to 150% on top of your phone's setting, sans or serif font, compact, normal or relaxed line spacing, images on or off
• Unread or all articles, mark all as read, folders, All articles and Starred

Staying up to date
• Refreshes when you open the app, and in the background when your phone allows it
• Choose a minimum time between background refreshes, from 15 minutes to 12 hours, and limit them to Wi-Fi if you like
• Asks each site whether its feed has changed before downloading it again, to save data

Keeping storage in check
• Keep the newest 25 to 500 articles per feed, and articles from the last 7 to 365 days
• Starred articles are never removed
• Delete read articles in one tap

Article text is stored for offline reading. Images load from the web when you're online.

Your privacy
{{NAME}} doesn't collect anything. There is no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. The app connects only to the feeds and websites you add, directly from your device, and to pages you choose to open. Nothing is sent to the developer. Everything stays on your phone, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

{{NAME}} is free, with no in-app purchases.

## Data safety answers

- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? No.
- Data collected: none. Data shared: none.
- The app has no accounts, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party SDKs that collect data. Subscriptions, articles and settings are stored only on the device.
