Purpose and audience
Hafnium Reader is an RSS/Atom feed reader for people who follow news sites, blogs and magazines and want to read them in one place, offline and without ads or tracking. It solves two problems: following many sites means visiting each one, and most news apps need a connection and show ads and pop-ups. The app downloads the articles of the feeds you choose to the phone, so they can be read anywhere, in a clean reader view with light, dark and sepia themes. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no user-generated content; it collects no data.

Setting up and using the main features (no login needed)
1. Launch the app. A fresh install has no feeds: tap "Start with sample feeds" on the Feeds screen. This subscribes to six public feeds (The Local Denmark, Ars Technica, The Verge, NASA, Quanta Magazine, xkcd) and downloads their articles.
2. Tap a feed (or "All articles") to see its articles, newest first. Tap an article to read it. Star it, share it, or move to the previous/next article.
3. For feeds that only give a summary, tap "Download full article"; the app saves the article's text for offline reading. "Open site" shows the original page with JavaScript off (no pop-ups).
4. Tap Add to subscribe to a site: type its name (for example "guardian"), paste its address, or paste a feed address such as https://xkcd.com/atom.xml.
5. Settings: appearance (theme, text size, font, line spacing), import or export subscriptions as an OPML file, offline storage limits, full-article downloads and background refresh.
6. To test offline reading, refresh with a connection, then turn on Airplane Mode and open articles.

External services
- The websites of the feeds the user subscribes to: the app downloads their public RSS/Atom feeds and, when asked, their article pages, directly from those sites.
- Wikidata (wikidata.org, Wikimedia Foundation): when the user searches Add by a site's name, the search words are sent to Wikidata's public API to find the site's web address.
There is no Hafnium server, no account or authentication service, no payments, no analytics or advertising SDK, and no AI service. Everything the app stores stays on the device.

Regional differences
The app works the same in every region. Content depends only on the feeds the user chooses. The app is not offered in mainland China.

Regulated industry or third-party material
Not applicable. The app is a general-purpose feed reader; it does not provide its own content. It shows public feeds and pages the user chooses, with links back to the original publisher, as other RSS readers do. The sample feeds are public RSS feeds the publishers offer for use in feed readers.
