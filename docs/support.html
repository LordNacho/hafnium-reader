Support

Need help with Hafnium Reader? Start with the questions below. If your answer isn't here, email info@hafnium.ltd and include your phone model and the feed address if a particular feed is involved.

Frequently asked questions

How do I move my subscriptions from The Old Reader?

On The Old Reader's website, go to Settings → Import/Export and choose Export as OPML. Save the file somewhere your phone can reach it, such as Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or your downloads, then import it into Hafnium Reader as described below.

Most other readers, including Feedly and Inoreader, also offer an OPML export in their settings.

How do I import an OPML file?

In Hafnium Reader, choose the option to import OPML and pick the file. Your feeds are added along with their folders, and the app then downloads the latest articles.

Why do some articles only show a summary?

Many feeds include just the first lines of each article. With Download full articles turned on in Settings (on Wi-Fi by default), Hafnium Reader downloads the linked page and keeps the readable text, so the whole article is there offline. In an article you can also tap Download full article, or switch back to the feed's summary.

Some articles stay as summaries: sites that load the rest of the text only when you tap a button, and paywalled articles. Use Open site for those.

Why does a website look broken when I tap Open site?

Hafnium Reader opens websites with JavaScript turned off, so timed pop-ups, cookie walls and most ads never appear. Some sites need JavaScript to show anything. Tap JS: Off at the top to turn it on for that site; Hafnium Reader remembers your choice. Browser opens the page in your phone's browser instead.

How much space does Hafnium Reader use?

As much as the Storage limit in Settings allows (100 MB to start with). When saved articles go over it, the oldest are removed, read ones first. Starred articles are always kept. Settings shows how much is in use.

How do I add a single feed?

Tap Add and type the site's name, for example Guardian or DR, to search the built-in directory. If it isn't there, tap Search the web to find the site by name (this sends your search words to Wikidata, Wikipedia's open database), or type its address, such as theguardian.com. Either way Hafnium Reader looks for the feeds the site offers and lets you choose. Searching the directory happens on your phone. RSS 2.0, RSS 1.0 (RDF) and Atom feeds are supported.

Why doesn't background refresh happen on a regular schedule?

Hafnium Reader refreshes every time you open it. Background refresh is different: iOS and Android decide when apps may run in the background, based on battery level, how often you use the app, network conditions and other factors. The interval you choose in settings is a minimum, not a guarantee, so refreshes may come later or less often than you set. If you turned on Wi-Fi only, background refresh waits for Wi-Fi. Low Power Mode or battery saver can also delay or pause it.

Why don't images show when I'm offline?

Article text is saved on your device, but images are loaded from the web each time you view them. Without a connection, articles still open but images won't appear. You can also turn images off in Appearance settings.

Where is my data, and how do I back it up?

Everything stays on your phone: your feeds, folders, articles, read and starred status, and settings. Nothing is stored on our side, so there is no account to restore from, and there is no sync between devices.

Your phone's own backup can include this data: iCloud Backup on iPhone, or Android's backup to your Google account. You control those backups in your phone's settings; we have no access to them.

To keep a separate copy of your subscriptions, use Export OPML and save the file with the share sheet (to Files, cloud storage or email). That file contains your feeds and folders only; articles, starred items and read status are not included.

Moving to a new phone

If you set up the new phone by restoring a backup of the old one (iCloud Backup on iPhone, or your Google account backup on Android), the app's data usually comes along.

Otherwise, on the old phone use Export OPML and send the file to yourself, then import it on the new phone. This moves your subscriptions and folders only; saved articles, starred items and read status aren't included. The app then downloads the latest articles from each feed.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Inside the app you can also use Delete read articles, or lower how many articles are kept per feed and for how many days. Starred articles are kept until you unstar them.

A feed won't load. What can I do?

Check that the address opens in your phone's browser. Some sites move or remove their feeds, and some block apps from fetching them. If a feed works in other readers but not in Hafnium Reader, email us the address.
